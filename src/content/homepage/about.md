## A bit about me

<columns>
<column ratio="53.19">
My path into product management wasn't particularly straightforward. I started in communications and brand strategy, ran my own business, then moved into technology and automation at Swedbank. Eventually, product management brought those experiences together.

Customer interviews and UX/design are among my favourite parts of the job. I enjoy asking that extra question that gets past the obvious answer, understanding why people behave the way they do, and working with designers to turn those insights into something useful and intuitive.

That background also helps me see a product from different sides. I understand why Marketing and Growth need flexibility, why Legal cares about risk, and what Engineering needs to deliver well. I enjoy bringing those perspectives together, especially when priorities compete.

And I care about the team behind the product. How people work together, what motivates them, and whether they have room to grow. I like coaching, sharing context and helping people become more confident in their decisions.
